How it works

No apps to install, no passwords to remember. One link on the clinic website does the rest.

1

Patient describes their need

A short guided form collects the concern, contact details, and how soon they feel they need a response.

2

The clinic is notified instantly

The request arrives structured and complete — no call-backs to fill gaps, no transcription from voicemail.

3

The team responds & tracks

Staff triage from the dashboard, contact the patient, and mark the request complete. Clinical judgement stays with the clinic.
