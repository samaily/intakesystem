What we do

Support that picks up where the system drops off.

We're not a clinic and we don't replace your doctor. We're the people who sit with you, read the paperwork, and make the calls you've been putting off.

Care planning

We take everything you've been handed — diagnoses, referrals, medication lists — and turn it into one plan written in plain language, with the next three things clearly marked.

Finding the right services

Home care, therapy, rehab, community programmes, benefits. We know what exists locally, what the waiting lists actually look like, and which options are worth your time.

Family support

Caregiving falls hardest on whoever picks up the phone first. We help families divide the work, talk about the hard parts, and stop running on adrenaline.

How it works

Four steps, in order, at your pace.

1

You email us

Write as much or as little as you want. A few lines about what's happened is enough to start. We reply within two business days.

2

We talk it through

A conversation by phone or video, usually about an hour. You describe the situation; we ask the questions that tend to get missed.

3

You get a written plan

Your options laid out, what each one involves, what it costs, and who to contact — with the steps ordered so you're never guessing which comes first.

4

We stay reachable

Plans meet reality and change. Check in when something shifts, and we'll adjust the next step rather than starting over.

Who we help

Most people reach us at one of these moments.

  • An older parent has come home from hospital and can't manage alone yet.
  • A diagnosis just landed and the follow-up appointment is weeks away.
  • Someone finished a treatment programme and needs the next stage in place.
  • A family is arguing about care because nobody has the full picture.
  • You're the one holding it all together and you're running out of road.
